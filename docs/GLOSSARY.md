# Glossary

## Core Concepts

- **Backtest** — Simulate a strategy over historical data.
- **Equity Curve** — Time series of portfolio value throughout a backtest.
- **Signal** — Trading instruction generated by a strategy (BUY, SELL, HOLD).

## Risk Metrics

- **Sharpe Ratio** — Risk-adjusted return: (return - risk-free rate) / volatility.
- **Sortino Ratio** — Like Sharpe but only penalizes downside volatility.
- **Max Drawdown** — Largest peak-to-trough equity decline during a backtest.
- **Win Rate** — Percentage of trades that were profitable.

## Technical Indicators

- **ATR (Average True Range)** — Volatility indicator measuring average price range. Used for position sizing and stop loss placement.
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** — Momentum oscillator (0-100) indicating overbought/oversold conditions.
- **SMA (Simple Moving Average)** — Average price over a fixed period.
- **EMA (Exponential Moving Average)** — Weighted moving average giving more weight to recent prices.
- **Wilder's Smoothing** — Exponential smoothing with alpha=1/period, used in ATR and RSI calculations.

## Position Sizing

- **Position Sizing** — Determining how many shares to buy/sell based on risk parameters.
- **Risk Per Trade** — Maximum percentage of equity to risk on a single trade.
- **ATR Multiplier** — Number of ATRs used to set stop loss distance.
- **Fixed Fractional** — Position sizing method using fixed percentage of equity.

## Transaction Costs

- **Commission** — Fee charged by broker per share or per trade.
- **Spread** — Difference between bid and ask price.
- **Slippage** — Difference between expected and actual fill price.
- **BPS (Basis Points)** — 1 bps = 0.01%. Used for spread/slippage measurement.
- **Volume Impact** — Price movement caused by large orders relative to average volume.

## Execution

- **Fill** — Execution of an order at a specific price.
- **Fill Logic** — Method for determining execution price (next_open, close, vwap).
- **VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)** — Average price weighted by volume.

## Data Architecture

- **Data Loader** — Component that fetches OHLCV data from a source.
- **Connection Pooling** — Reusing database connections for efficiency.
- **TTL (Time To Live)** — Cache expiration time in seconds.
- **Batch Loading** — Fetching multiple symbols in a single operation.

## Strategy Framework

- **Combination Method** — How multiple strategy signals are merged (OR, AND, PRIORITY, WEIGHTED).
- **Confirmation Logic** — Requiring multiple signals within a window before triggering.
- **Strategy Attribution** — Tracking which strategy generated each trade.

## Database

- **Hypertable** — TimescaleDB partitioned table for time-series data.
- **OHLCV** — Open, High, Low, Close, Volume - standard price bar format.

## Risk Management

- **Market Regime** — Macroeconomic state (bull / bear / neutral) derived from price vs. a long-period SMA (e.g. SPY 200-SMA).
- **Bull Gate** — Boolean flag that is True when the market regime allows new long entries. Mirrors RapidTrader's `bull_gate` column in `market_state`.
- **Stop Loss** — Pre-set price level at which a position is closed to limit further losses. Backgrid places stops at `entry_price - (ATR * multiplier)`.
- **Cooldown Period** — Number of trading days after a stop-loss trigger during which no new position may be opened in the same symbol. Prevents re-entering a trade immediately after a forced exit.
- **Portfolio Heat** — Aggregate capital at risk across all open positions, defined as the sum of `(entry_price - stop_price) * shares` per position. Expressed as a fraction of total portfolio value.
- **Heat Status** — Tiered classification of portfolio heat: COOL (<50 % of max), WARM (50-75 %), HOT (75-100 %), CRITICAL (>100 %).
- **Sector Limit** — Maximum allowed portfolio exposure to any single sector (default 30 %). Prevents concentration risk across correlated assets.

## Infrastructure

- **Idempotent** — Safe to retry without duplicate side-effects.
- **p95/p99** — 95th/99th percentile latency metrics.
- **HRP (Hierarchical Risk Parity)** — Portfolio method that clusters assets by correlation.
